About Sioufi Taxi

Our story, mission, and the team behind every ride.

Our Story

Sioufi Taxi was founded in Beirut with a simple but powerful mission: to provide safe, reliable, and affordable transportation for every Lebanese family and traveler. Starting with just two cars and a dream, we have grown into a trusted transportation company serving thousands of passengers across all of Lebanon.

Over more than a decade, we have built our reputation on punctuality, professionalism, and genuine care for our passengers. Every driver on our team is personally selected, trained, and committed to making your journey comfortable and safe.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To deliver safe, timely, and professional transportation services to every passenger across Lebanon, 24 hours a day.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted and recognized taxi brand in Lebanon, known for quality, reliability, and customer care.

Our Values

Safety first, honesty always, and a genuine commitment to every passenger's comfort and satisfaction.
